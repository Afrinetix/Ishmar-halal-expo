About Ishmar

48 expos, one mission: growing Kenyan business

We are the team behind the exhibitions, conferences and trade fairs that connect entrepreneurs, SMEs, investors and government institutions across Nairobi and Mombasa, and open access to the growing Halal economy.

Our story

Started in Nairobi, built for Kenyan business

Ishmar Halal Traders Expo Limited was registered in Nairobi in 2013 by a small group of traders who saw how much business Kenyan SMEs were losing simply because they had nowhere credible to meet buyers, investors and partners face to face. Our first expo, held in 2014, brought together a modest group of exhibitors and a few hundred visitors.

What changed things was simple: we treated exhibitors as our real customers, not just the visitors passing through. We built our processes around helping a food producer from Nairobi close a deal with a distributor from Mombasa, and word travelled fast.

Forty-eight expos later, Ishmar is a trusted exhibition and business networking platform operating in Nairobi and Mombasa, running trade fairs, corporate conferences, business expos and networking forums that connect entrepreneurs, SMEs, manufacturers, investors, government institutions, development organizations and international buyers.

Our vision

To become Africa's leading organizer of exhibitions, trade fairs, business networking events and Halal business platforms, connecting African businesses with regional and international markets.

Our mission

To create professional exhibition platforms that promote trade, investment, innovation, entrepreneurship and networking, while supporting sustainable business growth and access to the global Halal economy.

Our journey

Milestones

2013

Ishmar Halal Traders Expo Limited is registered in Nairobi by a small group of founding partners from the trade sector.

2014

Our first expo opens in Nairobi, connecting local exhibitors with early buyers and setting the format we still use today.

2017

Ishmar expands into Mombasa, bringing coastal SMEs and exporters into the same trade and networking platform.

2020

The expo adds a hybrid format, keeping exhibitors and buyers connected through a difficult trading period.

2023

Ishmar launches the Halal Investment Summit as a standalone conference, opening a dedicated channel between SMEs and investors.

2026

Ishmar successfully delivers its 48th expo, cementing its position as East Africa's trusted exhibition and business networking platform.

What we do

Everything it takes to fill a hall and close deals

Ishmar runs every part of the exhibition lifecycle in-house, from floor design to the last invoice, so exhibitors and sponsors get one point of contact from booking to breakdown.

Exhibition Mgmt Trade Shows Corporate Events Conferences Networking Sponsorship Marketing Branding Vendor Mgmt Registration

Exhibition Management

Full-service planning and delivery of trade exhibitions, from hall layout and exhibitor allocation to on-site operations and teardown.

  • Floor plan design and stand allocation
  • Venue sourcing and contractor coordination
  • On-site operations team for the full event

Trade Shows

Sector-specific showcases, from halal food and beverage to cosmetics and wellness, built around the buyers each industry actually needs.

  • Curated exhibitor mix by sector
  • Buyer-matching before doors open
  • Product sampling and demo zones

Corporate Events

Product launches, brand activations and private client days, produced with the same operational rigour as our flagship exhibitions.

  • Private venue sourcing
  • Guest list and VIP management
  • Brand-led staging and production

Conferences

Executive forums and summits, including the annual Halal Investment Summit, that put credible speakers in front of the right audience.

  • Speaker sourcing and programme design
  • Panel moderation and AV production
  • Delegate research and reporting

Business Networking

Structured B2B matchmaking sessions that turn a room of strangers into a working list of qualified introductions.

  • Pre-scheduled buyer-seller meetings
  • Delegation hosting for visiting missions
  • Post-event introduction reports

Sponsorship Management

Tiered sponsorship packages built around real visibility, from naming rights to speaking slots and delegate data.

  • Custom package design
  • Brand placement across print and digital
  • Post-event impact reporting

Event Marketing

Campaigns that fill the floor, combining trade media, digital advertising and direct outreach to our verified buyer database.

  • Digital and trade press campaigns
  • Access to our 40,000-contact buyer database
  • Exhibitor co-marketing toolkits

Branding

Stand design and identity work that helps exhibitors show up on the floor looking like the established brand they are.

  • Custom stand design and build
  • Signage, banners and print collateral
  • Digital brand assets for the event

Vendor Management

Logistics, catering, security and on-site contractor coordination handled by one team so exhibitors deal with a single contact.

  • Approved contractor network
  • Freight and customs support for exhibitors
  • On-site vendor supervision

Registration Management

Badge printing, QR check-in and real-time attendance data, so exhibitors know exactly who walked their stand.

  • Online pre-registration portal
  • QR badge scanning at every stand
  • Live visitor analytics dashboard

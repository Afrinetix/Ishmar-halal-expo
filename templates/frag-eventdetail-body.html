Ishmar Halal Trade Expo 2027

Three days, one hall, every part of the halal economy. Kenyatta International Convention Centre, Nairobi.

About this event

The Ishmar Halal Trade Expo is our flagship exhibition and the largest trade event in Kenya. Over three days, more than 350 exhibitors from food and beverage, cosmetics, finance, tourism and logistics set up across four halls at KICC, hosting pre-scheduled meetings with buyers from across the region.

Alongside the exhibition floor, the programme includes a conference stage running sector briefings, a dedicated investor lounge, and a matchmaking desk that books business meetings before the doors even open.

Who should attend

Exhibitors

Halal-certified brands ready to meet distributors, retailers and export buyers face to face.

Buyers & Distributors

Procurement teams sourcing new halal-certified suppliers across food, cosmetics and wellness.

Investors

Funds and DFIs scouting investable halal SMEs through our dedicated investor lounge.

Government & Policy

Trade agencies and county governments using the expo to brief exporters on new regulation.
